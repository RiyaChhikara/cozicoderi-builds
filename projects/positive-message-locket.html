Positive message locket

A round screen that keeps 50 small, positive messages close at hand.

Firmware compiled, not flashed
The build

What it is

A wearable locket built around the Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-1.28 round display. Its firmware holds 50 short messages and advances when the screen is tapped.

Why this form

Build intent

The idea is to make a positive prompt something you can touch and carry.

In plain terms

How it works

The ESP32-S3 runs the message firmware. A tap on the display moves to the next message. The firmware compiles, but it has not been flashed to the board yet.

Current status

Firmware compiled, not flashed

The firmware compiles with all 50 messages. Next is flashing it and checking the tap interaction on the actual board.
