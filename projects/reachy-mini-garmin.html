Garmin Venu 3S + Reachy Mini

A smartwatch and a small companion robot, brought together in an experiment that is still taking shape.

In progress · Device setup underway
The build

What it is

An experiment exploring what a Garmin Venu 3S and a Reachy Mini desktop companion can do together. Both devices are being set up.

Why this pairing

Build intent

I’m curious about what a smartwatch-led interaction with a small desktop robot could feel like, and whether it can make a useful, playful companion.

In plain terms

How it works

The project needs a working connection between the Garmin Venu 3S and the Reachy Mini. Device setup is under way, while the connection path and robot behaviour are still being explored.

Current status

In progress

Device setup is underway. A working connection between the watch and robot has not yet been confirmed.
