Three wearable cases

First-fit 3D printable forms for a locket, bracelet and magnet charm.

First-fit designs ready
The build

What it is

Three 3D printable case concepts for the Waveshare round display board: a locket, a bracelet and a magnet charm.

Why this form

Build intent

These three forms test how the same small screen can be worn or placed around a home.

In plain terms

How it works

Each case is shaped around the same board and screen. The case changes how the electronics are worn or mounted, while the display stays at the centre of the design.

Current status

First-fit designs ready

Three first-fit case options are ready. The designs are for the same round display board.
