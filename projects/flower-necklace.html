Flower necklace

A round screen at the centre of a flower, with physical petals around it.

In progress
The build

What it is

A wearable flower necklace in progress. The round display sits in the middle, surrounded by physical petals.

Why this form

Build intent

The goal is to make the screen part of a physical flower, with petals surrounding the display.

In plain terms

How it works

The ESP32-S3 round display forms the centre of the necklace. Physical petals extend around the screen to create the flower shape.

Current status

In progress

The flower form is still being developed. The screen stays at the centre, with physical petals around it.
