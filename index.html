A bit about me

Data by day, tiny devices after hours.

My work moves between data science, research, teaching and making. I’m interested in the point where a useful piece of technology starts to feel personal: a screen you can wear, a keyboard you can shape, or a computer you build around your own habits.

Follow along for build notes, hardware experiments and stories about the history behind everyday technology.
